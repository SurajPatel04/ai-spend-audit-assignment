## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no signup required to run the audit and see your results. We ask for your email only after you've seen the savings — never before.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
Every number in the audit traces back to the official pricing page of each vendor, verified the week of submission. The audit engine uses hardcoded rules — not AI guesses — for the math, so the calculations are deterministic and auditable. Sources are documented in our PRICING_DATA file.

**Q: What AI tools does the audit cover?**
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. These cover the vast majority of AI tool spend at early-stage startups as of mid-2026.

**Q: What does Credex do with my data?**
Your email and company details are stored securely and used only to send you the audit report and — for high-savings cases — to have a Credex consultant reach out about discounted AI credits. We do not sell your data. The public shareable URL strips all identifying information — only tool names and savings numbers are shown.

**Q: What is Credex and why are they offering this free?**
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — sourced from companies that overforecast their usage. The audit tool is free because it helps you understand your spend, and for teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex credits are often the best solution. If your audit shows under $100/month in savings, we'll still send you the report and notify you when better options become available for your stack.
