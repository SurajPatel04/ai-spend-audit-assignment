# ECONOMICS

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — sourced from companies that overforecast. Based on publicly available information about enterprise AI credit purchases and Credex's positioning:

- Average credit purchase: **$8,000–$15,000** (conservative estimate for a 10–40 person startup buying 6–12 months of AI credits upfront)
- Assumed gross margin on resale: **20–35%**
- Revenue per converted customer: **$1,600–$5,250**
- Using conservative midpoint: **~$2,500 gross profit per converted customer**

A customer who buys once and renews in 6 months is worth ~$5,000 over 12 months. Even a single-purchase customer at $2,500 gross profit justifies significant acquisition spend.

## Conversion Funnel Math

```
Landing page visitors
        ↓  65% start the form
Audit started
        ↓  70% complete it
Audit completed
        ↓  30% submit email (lead captured)
Lead captured
        ↓  10% book a Credex consultation
Consultation booked
        ↓  30% purchase credits
Credit purchase (revenue)
```

**End-to-end conversion: visitor → purchase**
= 0.65 × 0.70 × 0.30 × 0.10 × 0.30
= **~0.41%**

So roughly 1 in 240 visitors becomes a paying Credex customer.

**For the tool to be profitable from day one:**
- If a Show HN drives 500 visitors → ~2 purchases → ~$5,000 gross profit
- Zero cash spent to acquire those 500 visitors
- The tool pays for itself on the first HN post

**Minimum viable conversion rate to break even:**
If Credex spends $500 on light paid amplification (LinkedIn ads targeting CTOs), needs 1 sale to break even. At $500 CAC and $2,500 gross profit, breakeven is **1 sale per 5 paid conversions** — very achievable.

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**Target: $1M ARR = ~$83,000 revenue/month**

At $8,000 average deal size and 12-month contract:
- Need **~125 new customers over 18 months**
- That's **~7 new customers per month** by month 18

**Working backwards from the funnel:**

To get 7 purchases/month:
- Need 7 / 0.30 = **23 consultations/month**
- Need 23 / 0.10 = **230 leads/month**
- Need 230 / 0.30 = **767 audit completions/month**
- Need 767 / 0.65 = **~1,180 visitors/month**

**1,180 visitors/month is very achievable.** A single HN post drives that in one day. Sustained through Reddit, Discord seeding, and Credex's existing customer base, this is a realistic 6-month target, not an 18-month one.

What actually has to be true:

1. **Audit quality is trusted** — if the savings numbers feel made up, nobody books a consultation. The PRICING_DATA.md sourcing and defensible audit logic is directly tied to conversion rate.

2. **Credex sales team can close** — the tool generates warm leads but a human closes the deal. Consultation → purchase at 30% requires a competent sales conversation.

3. **Retention exists** — AI credit needs recur every 6–12 months. If customers rebuy, LTV doubles and the $1M ARR target becomes significantly easier to hit with the same acquisition volume.

4. **The shareable URL drives compounding traffic** — every shared audit result is a free ad. If 10% of audit completers share their result and each share drives 5 new visitors, the tool becomes partially self-distributing within 3 months.
